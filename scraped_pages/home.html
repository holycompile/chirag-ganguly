Chirag Ganguli is an accomplished security researcher, a Certified Ethical Hacker, and a graduate with a B.Tech degree in Computer Science and Engineering (Cyber Security and Digital Forensics) from the Vellore Institute of Technology. With a sharp focus on Security Automation and Cloud Security Infrastructure Management, Chirag brings a wealth of knowledge and experience to the table.

Over the past years, Chirag has excelled in his role as a Technical Lead in the Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), demonstrating his ability to lead and engage with others effectively. He has also completed internships with organizations such as the Madhya Pradesh Police and Virtually Testing Foundation, Hacktify, and Whizlabs, honing his skills in management and security roles.

With a background in cloud technologies such as Azure and Google Cloud, Chirag is currently gaining hands-on experience in Amazon Web Services, demonstrating his ability to adapt and learn quickly. He has also completed several online courses and boot camps in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning and Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing, showcasing his commitment to continuous learning and growth.

Chirag is passionate about networking and engaging with professionals in the field of Cyber Security, including core domains of Security Operations Center (SOC) and Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC). With his exceptional skills, experience, and dedication to his craft, Chirag is well-positioned to make a significant impact in the cybersecurity industry and beyond.

Additionally, Chirag is a Certified Lean Six Sigma Black Belt (LSSBB), which showcases his strong commitment to optimizing business processes and driving continuous improvement.|
